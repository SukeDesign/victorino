Business Suite

The single rooms in the Classic category offer very comfortable living. Between 15 m² and 24 m² in size, the majority of rooms are very spacious. These single rooms are equipped with a cosy seating corner, most have a desk, some have a walk-in wardrobe, and all have a safe or a locker, TV, telephone and Telekom W-LAN Internet access (free of charge). Some of the single rooms can be interconnected through internal doors. The bathrooms are equipped with a bathtub or shower, WC, cosmetic mirror, hairdryer and bath robe.
